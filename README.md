# HH Goa 2026 — Builder Identity Studio
Create your Hacker House Goa 2026 Builder ID Card and PFP Frame in seconds.

**Live site:** https://h-hgoa.vercel.app/

## About
A browser-based generator for two things: a Builder ID Card and a PFP Frame. Upload a photo, fill in your details, adjust the layout, and download the PNG, all from the browser. The look is Goa-inspired: forest green, cream, hot pink, and yellow.

## Features

**Builder ID Card** — upload a profile photo, add your name or handle, your stack or role, and a builder title. Position the photo, preview it live, and download the PNG.

**PFP Frame** — a square 1:1 frame with zoom and pan controls and a live preview. Download as PNG.

**Design** — palm trees and beach illustrations on a forest green and cream base, with hot pink and yellow accents. Works on mobile and desktop.

**Privacy** — images are processed client-side, in the browser. No account or signup needed.

## Tech Stack
React, Vite, JavaScript, CSS, HTML, Canvas for the image generation, deployed on Vercel.
